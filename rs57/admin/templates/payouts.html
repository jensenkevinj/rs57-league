What is stored here, and what is not

The prizes and winners above are derived — stats computes them and the nightly Action rewrites that file every run. Only the paid flag is a human fact, so only the paid flag is stored, in data/manual/payments.json. The amount is deliberately not copied there: it lives in payouts.json, and two copies of a number are two numbers.
