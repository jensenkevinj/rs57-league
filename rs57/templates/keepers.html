What every player on every roster would cost to keep in {{ current.season }}. Find your team, add up the players you want, then add the keeper fee for that many keepers.

How to read this

A keeper's price is his base plus the ${{ keeper_tax }} tax if you kept him last season. Base is what he cost you this season, not what you originally paid — every fee and tax you have paid on him is already inside it, which is why a player you have held for years is expensive.

{% if not current.drafted %}

{{ current.season }} has not been drafted yet, so a base here is last season's salary carried forward. That is the right number to make a keeper decision on; it is re-read after the auction.

{% endif %}
