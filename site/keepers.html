2026 keeper prices

What every player on every roster would cost to keep in 2026. Find your team, add up the players you want, then add the keeper fee for that many keepers.

How to read this

A keeper's price is his base plus the $5 tax if you kept him last season. Base is what he cost you this season, not what you originally paid — every fee and tax you have paid on him is already inside it, which is why a player you have held for years is expensive.

2026 has not been drafted yet, so a base here is last season's salary carried forward. That is the right number to make a keeper decision on; it is re-read after the auction.

The keeper fee is not in these prices. The fee depends on how many keepers you declare and you choose how to split it, so it cannot be worked out per player. Add it yourself:

Keepers declaredFee, in total
1 $0
2 $5
3 $15

Maximum 3 keepers plus 1 prospect. A prospect is kept at his base with no fee and no tax. There is no salary cap.
